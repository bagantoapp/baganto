About This Role

Baganto publishes this Grievance Officer in good faith as a small/early-stage platform. The heavier obligations that apply to "significant social media intermediaries" under the IT Rules, 2021 (such as appointing a separate Chief Compliance Officer, Nodal Contact Person, and filing monthly compliance reports) apply only once a platform crosses the registered-user thresholds defined in those Rules; we will appoint those additional roles if and when they become applicable to Baganto.

For data-protection-specific grievances under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, see also the Grievance Officer / Data Protection Contact section of our Privacy Policy.
