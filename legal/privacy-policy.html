2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Profile data: name, chosen avatar, and city you select when creating a profile.
  • Listing data: item titles, descriptions, photos (if you add any), category, price, and barter preferences you choose to post.
  • Trade & transaction data: trade proposals, in-app chat messages, ratings, and reviews you exchange with other users.
  • Technical data: basic app/device information needed to operate and sync the Platform (e.g. local storage on your device, and — if you use a synced/online mode — data sent to our backend server).

We do not currently collect precise GPS location, government-issued ID numbers, or payment/financial information, because the Platform does not process in-app payments. If this changes in a future version, we will update this Policy and the in-app consent flow accordingly.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the marketplace — showing your listings to other users, enabling trade proposals, chat, ratings, and (for sale listings) "Buy Now" requests;
  • To show an approximate distance between you and other users based on city-level location;
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the Platform;
  • To respond to support requests and enforce our Terms & Conditions;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Consent & Legal Basis

Creating a profile on Baganto is voluntary, and doing so is treated as your consent to this Policy as required under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your profile, though this may mean you can no longer use the Platform.

5. Who Can See Your Data

  • Other users can see your profile name, avatar, city, public listings, ratings/reviews, and any messages you send them as part of a trade.
  • Service providers who host our backend infrastructure, strictly to help us operate the Platform, and who are bound to protect your data.
  • Authorities, where required by law, regulation, court order, or a valid government request.

We do not otherwise share your personal data with third parties.

6. Where Your Data Is Stored

Depending on how the app is configured, your data is stored locally on your device (browser/app storage) and/or synced to our backend server so it stays consistent across your devices. We take reasonable security practices and procedures, as contemplated under the IT Rules, 2011, to protect data we hold — but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your profile is active. If you delete a profile using the in-app "Delete This Profile" option, that profile's listings, trades, messages, and ratings are permanently removed from active use. We may retain limited records for longer where required for legal, fraud-prevention, or audit purposes.

8. Your Rights

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct or update inaccurate or outdated personal data;
  • Erase personal data that is no longer necessary (including via in-app profile deletion);
  • Withdraw consent at any time;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity;
  • Lodge a grievance with our Grievance Officer (see Section 12) and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, use the relevant in-app feature (e.g. edit/delete profile) or contact us at [Support Email].

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created a profile, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Cookies & Local Storage

The app uses browser/device local storage to keep you signed in and to store app data on your device. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies; if that changes, we will update this Policy.

11. Cross-Border Data Storage

Where we use cloud infrastructure to host the backend, your data may be processed on servers located in India or, where applicable, in other countries, consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any government rules on cross-border transfer of personal data in force at the relevant time.
