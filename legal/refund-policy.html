Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026 · Template document — not legal advice. Replace placeholder details before relying on this for a real launch.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations, returns, and refunds work on Baganto for both barter trades and "Buy Now" sales arranged through the Platform.

2. Barter Trades

  • A trade proposal can be withdrawn (by the sender) or rejected (by the recipient) at any time before it is accepted, at no cost, directly within the app.
  • Once both users mark a trade "Complete," it is considered final on the Platform. Any return of items after that point must be agreed directly between the two users — Baganto does not mediate the physical return of bartered goods.

3. "Buy Now" Sales — Important Disclosure

Baganto is a peer-to-peer facilitator, not the seller of any item, and does not process or hold payment. Payment and item handover happen directly between buyer and seller (for example, cash or UPI at an agreed meeting point), unless a specific in-app payment feature states otherwise at the time. Because Baganto does not handle the money, Baganto itself cannot issue refunds — any refund must be arranged directly between buyer and seller.
  • Before handover, either party may cancel a pending purchase within the app at no cost.
  • After handover, returns or refunds are at the discretion and mutual agreement of the buyer and seller, since the transaction occurs directly between them. We encourage sellers to describe items accurately and honor reasonable return requests, and buyers to inspect items carefully before completing a purchase.

4. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

Baganto operates as a marketplace connecting individual buyers and sellers, rather than selling items itself. Consistent with the marketplace model under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, individual sellers using the Platform are responsible for the accuracy of their own listings and for honoring fair grievance redressal towards buyers for their own sales. Baganto will reasonably assist in resolving disputes raised in good faith, including by suspending profiles found to be engaging in fraudulent sales.

5. Resolving a Dispute

  1. Use the in-app chat on the relevant trade to try to resolve the issue directly with the other user.
  2. If unresolved, contact us at [Support Email] with the trade details.
  3. For complaints that remain unresolved, you may escalate to our Grievance Officer — see our Grievance Redressal page.

6. Fraud & Suspicious Transactions

If you believe you have been the victim of a fraudulent listing or trade, report it immediately to [Support Email]. We may suspend any profile involved in suspected fraud and, where appropriate, assist you in reporting the matter to the relevant authorities. Because payments are not processed through the Platform, we cannot guarantee recovery of funds or items exchanged off-platform.

7. Future Changes

If Baganto introduces integrated in-app payments in the future, this Policy will be updated with specific refund timelines (for example, refund to original payment method within a stated number of business days) before that feature goes live.
